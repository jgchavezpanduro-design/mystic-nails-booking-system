## FAQ about Builder Gel

**What's the difference between Builder Gel and Rubber Base?**
Builder Gel gives more structure and support, while Rubber Base is more flexible and natural. Both reinforce the nail, but with a different level of firmness.

**Is Builder Gel an extension?**
No, it's applied over the natural nail to add strength, not to lengthen it.
