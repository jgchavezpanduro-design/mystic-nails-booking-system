## FAQ about Kapping

**What is Kapping?**
It's a technique that covers the natural nail with polygel or acrylic overlay to give it more protection and durability.

**Does Kapping add length?**
It adds a bit of length and structure, but not as much as a full extension like Soft Gel or Acrylics.
