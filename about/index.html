Our story

Mystic Nails Art opened in Playa del Carmen in 2023 with a simple goal: bring salon-quality nail art and technique to a beach town full of both locals and travelers. What started as a small private studio has grown into a name that more than 1,300 clients recognize for consistent quality, hygiene, and design.

We specialize in Gelish, Rubber Base, Builder Gel, Kapping, nail extensions (Soft Gel, Gel X, acrylic, and polygel), manicure, pedicure, and fully custom nail art. Every service is built around the same idea: results that look intentional and last, not just for a few days but for weeks.

Why clients choose us

  • Experience: specialized in nail art and gel techniques since 2023, with more than 1,300 clients served
  • Hygiene: certified quality products and strict sterilization between every client
  • Bilingual service: we book and work in Spanish and English, for both locals and tourists
  • Easy booking: appointments are confirmed directly on WhatsApp, no app or account needed

Who we serve

Mystic Nails Art works with local clients from Playa del Carmen and Quintana Roo, as well as tourists visiting the Riviera Maya who want their nails ready for the beach, a wedding, or a special event. We're used to coordinating appointments around travel schedules and can usually confirm same-day or next-day availability by WhatsApp.

We're located at Calle 38 Nte Lote 73, Tohoku, Centro, Playa del Carmen, Quintana Roo, open Monday through Sunday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, by appointment. See our contact page for the map and phone number, or go straight to booking.
