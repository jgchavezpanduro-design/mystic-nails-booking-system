FAQ about Soft Gel / Gel X

What's the difference between Soft Gel/Gel X and acrylics?

Soft Gel/Gel X is lighter and more comfortable, ideal for a natural finish. Acrylics offer more structure for elaborate designs or longer lengths.

Does the application hurt?

No, it's a painless process that adapts to the natural shape of your nail.
