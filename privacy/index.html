Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Who we are

Mystic Nails Art is a nail salon located at Calle 38 Nte Lote 73, Tohoku, Centro, Playa del Carmen, Quintana Roo, Mexico. This page explains what information mysticnailsart.com collects from visitors, how it's used, and how to reach us about it.

Booking and WhatsApp

Mystic Nails Art doesn't have a server-side booking database. When you use the "Book Appointment" or "Quote" forms on this site, the details you enter (name, service, date, time, notes) are used only to prepare a message that opens directly in WhatsApp — they are not stored on our website or sent to any server we control before that point.

Once you send that message, the conversation happens on WhatsApp and is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. We keep the appointment details you share on WhatsApp only as needed to schedule and provide your service.

Analytics and advertising

  • Google Analytics & Google Ads: we use Google's tagging (gtag.js) to understand site traffic and measure the results of our ads. This sets cookies and may share standard analytics data (like pages visited and approximate location) with Google.
  • Google Fonts: this site loads fonts from Google's servers (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) to display our typography, which involves your browser connecting to Google to fetch those files.

We don't sell your personal information to third parties, and we don't use any tracking beyond the standard Google Analytics/Ads setup described above.

Cookies

This site uses cookies set by Google Analytics and Google Ads for the purposes described above. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time; doing so won't affect your ability to browse the site or book an appointment on WhatsApp.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to delete a past conversation or appointment record, or ask general questions about this policy at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp at +52 984 310 8186.

Changes to this policy

If the way we handle visitor information changes meaningfully, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.
