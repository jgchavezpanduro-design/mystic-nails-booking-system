FAQ about Gelish

How long does Gelish last?

Gelish lasts more than 3 weeks with a shiny finish and no chipping, as long as you follow the recommended care.

What's the difference between Gelish and Rubber Base?

Gelish is a gel polish for natural nails focused on shine and color. Rubber Base is a reinforcement designed for weak nails, with a more natural, flexible finish.

Can I get a design or nail art over Gelish?

Yes, Gelish supports nail art and custom designs. The final quote may vary depending on the design and its complexity.
